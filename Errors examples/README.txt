The examples in this folder show a wide variety of error conditions during the various passes of the assembler.

There are a few more individual error conditions that the assembler will report, and are not shown in the examples here.

I have divided the examples according to the reading order in which the assembler reads:

- Errors in the pre-assembler stage (macro definition and deployment)

- Basic syntax errors, at the beginning of the first pass stage.

- Errors in the middle of the first pass, such as incorrectly defining labels, or using arguments that are not allowed in the various operations, and a wide variety more..

- Errors in the second pass, such as using labels that have not been defined, or recognizing declarations on entry statements that have not been implemented in the source code.
